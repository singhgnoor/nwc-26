## What the Dean and his panel asked for
- **Not like the previous editions' pages.** IIT Hyderabad's 2024 page and IIT Bombay's 2025 page were near-empty write-ups with no schedule, speakers, contacts or photos. The panel called them pages that exist "just for the sake of it".

- **National-level and high-stakes, and it must not look like a corporate website.** Warm, human and dignified. Something a Minister's office opens and takes seriously.
- **Interactive programme.** Each talk or panel in the schedule can be **hovered or clicked to expand**, showing the speakers and panellists with **photos**, name, designation and institution. On mobile, tap to expand (no hover).
- **Photos section.** "Coming soon" until the event, then a **hand-picked carousel** of event photos. Not just a Drive link.
- **Feedback.** A clear place for the **Google Form** feedback link (required).

## Visual direction (my constraints; you propose the rest)
- **Align with the Ministry's campaign so the portal reads as its official home.** The MoE launch posts use warm cream/off-white backgrounds, coral-red → orange headings, deep navy, mustard-yellow highlights, teal/green accents and thin gold corner-frame lines. Keep this family of colours.
- **More refined than the posts.** No cartoon brains or clip-art. If there's illustration, make it calm and crafted, e.g. line work, organic shapes, or something drawn from the place.
- **Make it about the place.** IIT Ropar sits on the Sutlej with the Shivalik foothills behind it, and its main gate has four stone-carved pillars on a square. A signature motif tied to "ascent" or "journey" fits the theme and the series story.
- **Readable for a senior audience.** Generous type sizes, strong contrast (WCAG AA minimum), and clear hierarchy. No light-grey body text.
- **Typography.** A characterful but dignified pairing, chosen as **Latin + Devanagari pairs** from Google Fonts, so the Ministry's Devanagari name and tagline render properly. Devanagari runs longer, so don't size headings, buttons, tags and session cards so tightly that longer text breaks them.
- **National emblem.** Use it unaltered, with its standard text and correct proportions. No recolouring, cropping or effects.
- **Avoid these:** generic SaaS look, gradients everywhere, stock photos of people meditating, glassmorphism, dark mode as the default.
